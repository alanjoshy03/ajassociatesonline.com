For small businesses

Small business (proprietorship)

For a small business or sole trader who needs the basics done properly.

  • Annual Income Tax Return (ITR-3/4 presumptive) filing
  • Financial statements (P&L and balance sheet)
  • Form 26AS / AIS reconciliation and TDS claiming
  • Advance tax computation and payment schedule advice
  • MSME / Udyam registration and compliance support
  • Bank statement reconciliation and ledger review

We review every file before it goes out, and we deal with it if the department asks questions.

What it costs

Priced around your business

There is no fixed price list, because no two businesses need the same work. Your fee depends on what you need done, your turnover and how many transactions you have. We recommend only what you actually need, and we tell you the fee in writing before we start.

You can also begin small. Many clients start with a single service, such as a registration or a return, and add more later if they want to. Tell us about your business and we will send you a clear figure.
