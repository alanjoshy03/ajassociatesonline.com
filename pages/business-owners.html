For business owners. Support all year, not just filings.

Accounting, tax and compliance handled through the year: books kept up to date, returns filed on time and notices dealt with when they come.

Book an appointment Find your package

What we look after

Your accounts, tax and compliance, all year

  1. 01

    Accounting and monthly reports

    Day-to-day bookkeeping, bank reconciliations and a monthly management report (MIS) showing sales, costs and profit, so you can see where the business stands.

  2. 02

    GST, TDS and income tax

    Returns filed on time, with due dates tracked on a compliance calendar, so you are not chasing deadlines.

  3. 03

    Payroll, PF and ESI

    Salary calculation, payslips, provident fund and ESI filings and labour-law compliance.

  4. 04

    Notices and assessments

    If a notice arrives, we read it, draft the reply and represent you before the authorities.

  5. 05

    Loans and funding

    Financial projections and the papers banks ask for, and support through their questions.

  6. 06

    Advice as you grow

    Restructuring, valuation, mergers and scaling up, discussed before decisions are made.

How we work with you

  • Reports you can use

    Monthly or weekly reports, with the figures and analysis you ask for, delivered on time.

  • Easy to reach

    WhatsApp, phone or email. We usually reply straight away on working days. On Sundays and holidays we still reply, but more slowly.

  • A team for each area

    Tax, GST, accounts and audit, company law and loans each have their own team, so your work goes to the people who do that kind of work every day.

Situations we often help with

  • Your GST returns and your books don’t agree.We match the purchases and sales, find the gaps, and set up a monthly check so it doesn’t happen again.
  • A bank has asked for projections for a loan.We prepare the financial statements and projections in the format banks use, and support you through their questions.
  • You are not sure whether to be a company or an LLP.We compare the options against your plans, and explain the costs and the paperwork of each.
  • A notice has arrived and nobody has replied yet.We read it, prepare the reply and appear for you if needed.

Already have an accountant?

That is quite common. Some clients come to us for everything, and some only for the parts they need extra help with. We will work out with you what makes sense, and we can hand things over gradually if you want to.

  1. 01

    We look at what you have

    Your books, returns and registrations, from your accountant or from the portals.

  2. 02

    We see what is pending

    Filings still to be made, and anything overdue.

  3. 03

    We note what is missing

    Documents, statements or entries that still need to be completed.

  4. 04

    We check the books against your records

    The bank, GST and your own papers, so the figures agree.

  5. 05

    We look for open notices

    Anything outstanding on the tax or GST portals.

  6. 06

    We agree who does what

    Then a calendar of your due dates, and we take on only the work you want us to.
