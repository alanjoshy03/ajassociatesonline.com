We work with businesses of every kind.

Our clients range from people starting their first business to families who have run theirs for generations.

  • Manufacturing and trading

    From workshops and factories to wholesalers and distributors, we keep the books, GST and statutory filings in step with your production and stock.

    What usually comes up: Stock that doesn’t match the books, and input tax credit spread across many purchase bills.

    • Manufacturing
    • Wholesale
    • Distribution
    • Dealers
    • Supply chains

  • Retail and e-commerce

    Shops, showrooms and online sellers get clean GST returns, stock-aware accounts and clear margins on every line you sell.

    What usually comes up: Marketplace payments that don’t match your sales, and GST on returns and refunds.

    • Shops
    • Showrooms
    • Online sellers
    • Marketplaces

  • Healthcare and education

    Hospitals, clinics, labs, schools and training institutes: compliance that respects how your institution actually runs.

    What usually comes up: A mix of taxable and exempt income, and TDS on many small payments.

    • Hospitals
    • Clinics
    • Laboratories
    • Schools
    • Colleges
    • Training institutes

  • Hospitality and tourism

    Hotels, resorts, restaurants and travel businesses: seasonal cash flow, service charges and multiple tax rates handled cleanly.

    What usually comes up: Different GST rates on rooms, food and services, and cash flow that swings with the season.

    • Hotels
    • Resorts
    • Restaurants
    • Homestays
    • Travel agencies

  • Real estate and construction

    Builders, developers and contractors: project-wise accounts, work-contract GST and TDS that stand up to scrutiny.

    What usually comes up: Project-wise accounts, GST and TDS on work contracts, and advances received from buyers.

    • Builders
    • Developers
    • Contractors
    • Property owners

  • IT and start-ups

    Software firms, agencies and first-generation start-ups: clean books, founder-friendly structures and investor-ready compliance.

    What usually comes up: Payments from abroad, founder pay and shareholding, and papers that investors ask for.

    • Software
    • Agencies
    • Start-ups
    • Freelancers
    • SaaS

  • Agriculture, seafood and exports

    Plantations, farms, processors and exporters: agricultural income, export incentives and cross-border paperwork in order.

    What usually comes up: Agricultural income, export refunds, and import-export paperwork.

    • Plantations
    • Farms
    • Seafood
    • Spices
    • Importers and exporters

  • Finance, professionals and individuals

    NBFCs, chit funds, doctors, lawyers, architects, trusts, NRIs and families: advice shaped around who you are.

    What usually comes up: Several sources of income, capital gains, and accounts for trusts or Hindu Undivided Families.

    • NBFCs
    • Chit funds
    • Professionals
    • Trusts
    • NRIs
    • Families and HUFs
