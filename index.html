Questions we hear every week.

Here are three of the most common ones, with straight answers. There are plenty more in our FAQs.

01
Salaried & professionals

“Which ITR form should I file? I’m completely confused.”

Filing under the wrong form can cost you a refund. Salary, freelance income, capital gains and trading each follow different rules. Share your Form 26AS and AIS with our tax team and we’ll handle the return for you.

Read more
02
Small businesses & startups

“Do I really need a GST registration for my business?”

It depends, and we’ll tell you straight. Your turnover, whether you sell to other states and what your customers expect all matter. Registering early can save you tax, or it can just add paperwork. Tell us how you sell and we’ll say what makes sense.

Read more
03
Notices & scrutiny

“I received an Income Tax / GST notice. Am I in trouble?”

Don’t panic. You don’t have to deal with the department alone. Many notices come from small mismatches in the numbers. Send us yours in confidence and we’ll check it, write the reply and appear for you if needed.

Our services

Not sure where to start?
01Tax, GST & Compliance

Fewer surprises at tax time. We handle your GST, income tax and TDS filings, plan your advance tax, and speak for you if a notice arrives.

  • GST, Income Tax & TDS filingsIncome tax returns (ITR), GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and the annual return) and quarterly TDS/TCS returns.
  • PF, ESI & payroll complianceMonthly provident fund (PF) and ESI returns, professional tax and other labour-law filings.
  • Assessments & scrutinyIncome tax and GST assessments and departmental audits. We write the replies and appear for you.
  • Compliance trackingA calendar of your due dates, checked regularly, so nothing slips and turns into a penalty.

02Business Registration

Start on the right footing. We help you choose the structure, register the business and prepare the founding documents.

  • Incorporation & registrationPrivate Limited, LLP, One Person Company, partnership firms and proprietorships.
  • Registrations & licencesPAN, TAN (for TDS), digital signatures (DSC), director ID (DIN), Import Export Code and MSME Udyam.
  • MOA, AOA & deedsThe founding documents of a company (MOA and AOA), partnership deeds and shareholder agreements, drafted to protect the founders.
  • Post-incorporationPapers for your first board meeting, help with the bank account, and getting you ready for your first annual filings.

Know where your business stands. Bookkeeping, monthly reports and independent audits that show how the business is really doing.

  • Bookkeeping & ledgersDay-to-day recording, voucher management and bank reconciliations.
  • Financial statements & MISBalance sheet, profit and loss account, and a monthly management report (MIS) showing sales, costs and profit.
  • Cash flow & fund flowBudgets, and a clear picture of the money coming in and going out.
  • Internal & process auditsIndependent audits, and check-ups of your finances and of how money is handled in your business.

A stronger case for your loan. We prepare the financial projections and papers banks ask for, and support you through their questions.

  • DPR & project reportsA detailed project report (DPR) and business plan for your funding application.
  • OD/CC & term loansApplication processing for overdraft or cash-credit limits (day-to-day working capital) and term loans for expansion.
  • Lender checks & credit ratingHelp when the lender looks into your business, and advice on improving your credit rating.
