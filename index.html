Salaried & consultants

“Which ITR form should I file? I’m completely confused.”

Filing under the wrong form can cost you a refund. Salary, freelance income, capital gains and trading each follow different rules. Share your Form 26AS and AIS with our tax team and we’ll handle the return, error-free.

Get ITR guidance
02
Small businesses & startups

“Do I really need a GST registration for my business?”

It depends — and we’ll say so plainly. Turnover, inter-state sales and what your clients expect all matter. Voluntary registration can save tax or simply add paperwork. Tell us how you operate and we’ll give honest advice.

Check my GST eligibility

Notices & scrutiny

“I received an Income Tax / GST notice. Am I in trouble?”

Take a breath — you don’t face the department alone. Many notices come from small ledger mismatches. Share yours in confidence and our senior team will verify it, draft the response and represent you before the authorities.
