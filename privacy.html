AJ Associates (“the Firm”, “we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes the personal information we collect through ajassociatesonline.com (“the Website”) and through our communications with you, the purposes for which we use it, the circumstances in which we disclose it, and the rights available to you. By using the Website or submitting your details to us, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy.

Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to visitors to the Website, persons who enquire about or request our services, applicants for positions, and prospective collaborators. Information we receive in the course of an engagement with a client is additionally governed by the terms of that engagement and by our professional duty of confidentiality.

Email: info@ajassociatesonline.com
Telephone: +91 62824 06091

Information we collect

  • Information you provide. Your name, mobile number, email address, the service in which you are interested, your message, and any documents or details you choose to share when you contact us, request an appointment, propose a collaboration or apply for a position.
  • Communications. Records of correspondence with us by email, telephone, messaging applications or in person.
  • Technical information. Standard information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of access, collected automatically for security and operational purposes.

We do not request passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), card details, PINs or online banking credentials through the Website or by message. Please do not share them with us.

Purposes of processing

  • Responding to your enquiries and requests, and scheduling consultations.
  • Providing, administering and improving our professional services.
  • Evaluating job applications and collaboration proposals.
  • Complying with applicable legal, tax, accounting and professional obligations, including record-keeping.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorised access, and maintaining the security and proper functioning of the Website.
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Basis of processing and consent

We process your personal information on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box and submitting a form or by contacting us voluntarily; to respond to a request made by you; or for a legitimate use permitted by law, including compliance with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time as described below. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Disclosure to third parties

We disclose personal information only to the extent necessary and only to:

  • service providers that support our operations, such as website, communications and email service providers, under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations;
  • professional advisers, auditors and associates who are bound by a duty of confidentiality;
  • government, tax or regulatory authorities, courts and law-enforcement agencies where required by law or legal process; and
  • a successor to our practice, in the event of a restructuring or transfer, subject to equivalent protection of your information.

We require our service providers to process personal information only on our instructions and to protect it appropriately.

Cross-border processing

Some of our service providers may process or store information on servers located outside India. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure that your information continues to be protected, and we do so in accordance with applicable law.

Cookies and third-party services

The Website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Certain embedded third-party content, such as Google Maps, may set its own cookies or collect technical information when it is loaded. Links to third-party platforms, including social networks and messaging applications, take you to services governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for the practices of third parties, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, and for such further period as is required by applicable tax, accounting, corporate and professional regulations. Enquiries that do not result in an engagement are retained for a limited period and then deleted. Information is securely deleted or anonymised once it is no longer required.

Security

We maintain reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, appropriate to the nature of the information, to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access is restricted to persons who need it for the purposes described above. No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we therefore ask that you share only the information necessary until an engagement has been agreed.

Personal data breaches

In the event of a personal data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority in the manner and within the time required by applicable law.

Your rights

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you have the right to:

  • obtain a summary of the personal data we process and the processing activities undertaken;
  • request correction, completion, updating or erasure of your personal data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • have access to readily available means of grievance redressal; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@ajassociatesonline.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal, and certain records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations, in which case we will tell you.

Your responsibilities

Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and, where you provide information relating to another person, that you are authorised to do so.

Children

The Website is intended for adults and business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under 18 years of age. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

The Website may contain links to external websites. This Policy does not apply to them, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law or in our practices. The revised version takes effect when it is published on the Website, and the date at the top indicates when it was last updated. Your continued use of the Website after publication constitutes acknowledgement of the revised Policy.

Governing law

This Policy is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Kochi, Kerala have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.

Grievances and complaints

Any concern or complaint relating to the processing of your personal information, the Website or our services may be submitted to the Grievance Officer, AJ Associates, at the address above or by email to info@ajassociatesonline.com or by telephone to +91 62824 06091. Please include your name, contact details and a description of the matter, together with any relevant reference.

We will acknowledge your complaint within 7 days of receipt and endeavour to resolve it within 30 days. If you remain dissatisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India or any other authority having jurisdiction under applicable law.
