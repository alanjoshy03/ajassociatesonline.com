# AJ Associates website: beta test list

Thank you for trying the new website. It takes about 15 minutes. Please try it on your **phone**, and on a
**laptop** if you can. You are looking for anything that is broken, confusing, slow, hard to read or looks odd.

## Ten things to try

1. **First impression (Home).** Open the website. Does the first screen fit and read well? Wait a few seconds: the
   box on the right changes between three cards. Use its small arrows too.
2. **Send an enquiry (Contact page).** Fill in the form and press "Send enquiry". You should see a thank-you
   message on the page. Also try leaving a required box empty.
3. **Book a consultation (Contact page, second tab).** Try to pick a Sunday and Friday 2 October 2026 (both should be
   refused with a message). Then pick a normal weekday, a time and how you would like to meet, and send it.
4. **The dropdowns.** Open every dropdown (service, time, how to meet). Choose an option and check it stays selected.
   On a phone, is the list easy to tap?
5. **FAQs.** Search for "GST". Open a few questions. Then go back to Home and press "Read the answer" under one of the
   three questions: it should open that exact answer.
6. **Resources.** Switch the deadline calendar between Monthly, Quarterly and Annual. Tick items on a checklist and
   watch the progress line. Swipe the pinned notes sideways.
7. **Services.** Go through the seven services (hover on a laptop, tap on a phone). Open one. Then find
   "Industries we serve" and scroll through the cards.
8. **Packages and the tax estimate.** On Packages, choose a business type and turnover and read the result. On
   Home, move the income slider on the "Quick tax estimate" card. Do the numbers look sensible?
9. **Company structure guide.** On the Company Formation service page, take the short guide. Does the result make sense
   for the answers you gave?
10. **Links and buttons.** Try every item in the menu, the footer links, the phone number, the email address, the
    WhatsApp button and the small chat button. Then type a wrong address (for example `/abc`) to see the "page not
    found" page.

## Also tell us

- Did anything feel slow, jumpy or heavy on your phone?
- Was anything hard to read (too small, too faint, too cramped)?
- Did any words, dates or numbers look wrong, or any spelling mistakes?
- Was there anything you could not find, or did not understand?

## How to report something

Please send a **screenshot**, and these four things:

1. The device (for example "Samsung Galaxy A52" or "iPhone 13" or "Windows laptop")
2. The browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
3. The page (for example "Contact, booking tab")
4. What you did, and what you expected to happen
