AJ Associates (“the Firm”) is committed to ensuring that ajassociatesonline.com (“the Website”) is accessible to all visitors, including persons with disabilities, in line with the principles of equality and inclusion and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. This statement describes our approach, the measures we have taken and how you can reach us if you face a difficulty.

Our commitment

We aim to present content in a clear, consistent and perceivable manner, to support the use of assistive technologies, and to review the Website periodically so that accessibility continues to improve. We work towards conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, which is widely recognised as the benchmark for accessible web content.

Measures taken

  • Logical page structure, headings and navigation landmarks, with a link to skip directly to the main content.
  • Navigation and controls that can be operated from the keyboard, with visible focus indication.
  • Text and background colour combinations selected for adequate contrast.
  • Descriptive labels, instructions and error messages for form fields.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images, and decorative images marked so that they are ignored by assistive technology.
  • A responsive layout that adapts to different screen sizes, orientations and text sizes.
  • Reduced motion for visitors whose device is set to limit animation.
  • Plain, consistent language and clear link text.

Compatibility

The Website is designed to work with current versions of widely used browsers on desktop and mobile devices, and with assistive technologies such as screen readers and screen magnifiers. It supports browser zoom and text enlargement without loss of content.

Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. Third-party content, such as the embedded map, is outside our control and may not meet the same standard. Some documents shared with clients may be provided in formats that require separate assistance. If you are unable to access any information or service, please contact us and we will provide it in an alternative format or assist you directly.

Ongoing review

We review the Website periodically, and when we make significant changes, to identify and correct accessibility barriers. This statement will be updated as improvements are made.

Feedback and assistance

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the Website. To report a difficulty or request information in another format, write to info@ajassociatesonline.com or call +91 62824 06091, giving the page concerned, a description of the problem and the assistive technology you use, if any. We aim to respond within 7 days.
