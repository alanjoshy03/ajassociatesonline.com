We start by listening,
and we see it through.

Four steps, every time, so you always know what is done and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We ask about your business, your books and whatever is worrying you, before we suggest anything.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We go through your filings, Form 26AS and AIS and your ledgers, and tell you early about any risks or savings we find.

  3. 03

    Execute

    We file the returns, register what needs registering, reconcile the books and draft the replies, and someone checks every file before it goes out.

  4. 04

    Watch

    We keep a calendar of your due dates and check in with you regularly, so penalties and surprises don’t catch you out.
