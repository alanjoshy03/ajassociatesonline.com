This policy explains what personal information AJ Associates (“we”, “us”) collects through ajassociatesonline.com, how we use it and the choices you have. We keep it short and clear on purpose.

Who we are

AJ Associates is a tax, audit and management consultancy based at Second Floor, 10/1329 G, Bivera, Chullickal Road, Chullickal, Kochi, Kerala – 682006. You can reach us at info@ajassociatesonline.com or +91 81368 85152.

What we collect

  • Details you send us. When you submit the enquiry or collaboration form, or email us, message us on WhatsApp, call us or apply for a role, we receive what you choose to share: your name, mobile number, email address, the service you are interested in, your message and any documents you attach by email.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

We do not ask for passwords, OTPs, card details or bank login details through this website, and you should never send them to us in a form or message.

How the forms work

When you submit a form, your details are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the form-handling service built into our hosting platform. That service stores the submission on its servers and forwards a copy to our email inbox, and our team replies from there. The forms include a hidden anti-spam field, and the website itself runs no database of its own. If you would rather not use a form, you can email, call or message us on WhatsApp instead.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and to provide and manage our services.
  • To assess applications and collaboration proposals you send us.
  • To meet our legal, tax, accounting and professional record-keeping obligations.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and similar technologies

At the time of writing, this website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The office map on our Contact page is provided by Google and may set its own cookies when it loads. Links to LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own policies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this page.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where needed: with service providers that help us run our business (such as our website hosting, form-handling and email services), with professionals who work with us under a duty of confidence, and with authorities where the law requires it. We do not share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was given, and for as long as tax, accounting and other laws or professional standards require us to keep client records. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are kept for a reasonable period and then deleted.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, and the website is served over HTTPS. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please share only what is needed and avoid sending sensitive documents until we have agreed to work together.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw your consent to our using it. Write to info@ajassociatesonline.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Some records we must keep by law, and we will tell you if that applies.

Children

This website is meant for adults and businesses. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Grievances and complaints

If you have a question or concern about how we handle your personal information, or any other complaint about this website or our services, write to info@ajassociatesonline.com or to us at the address above, marked for the attention of the Managing Partner (grievance contact).
